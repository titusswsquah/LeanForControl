\chapter{State estimation of unstabilizable linear systems}

This chapter tracks the formalization of \emph{State estimation of
unstabilizable linear systems: the optimization perspective, Part~II --- the
value-function route}. The target is the stability dichotomy: the
full-information estimator is globally asymptotically stable iff C1 and C2
hold, and globally exponentially stable iff C1, C2, and C3w hold.

Nothing is formalized yet. The Lean-side scaffold is
\texttt{LeanForControl/Estimation/Basic.lean} (conventions only); the
dependency audit and suggested order of attack live in
\texttt{notes/costogo-formalization-plan.md}.

\section{Standing conditions}

The three conditions on the pair $(A, C)$ and the noise-input matrix $G$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{C1} --- $(A, C)$ is detectable.
  \item \textbf{C2} --- $\ker \Sigma_0$ meets the unstable uncontrollable
        subspace of $(A, G)$ only at zero.
  \item \textbf{C3w} --- $(A, G)$ has no uncontrollable eigenvalues on the
        unit circle.
\end{itemize}
